# Vocabulary and collocations

[← Lesson overview](lesson.md)

## Health and treatment

- `a splint`: шина или фиксатор для повреждённой части тела.
- `an orthopaedic doctor` (BrE) / `an orthopedic doctor` (AmE).
- `a prescription for a gel`.
- `reduce inflammation / swelling`.
- Example: `The doctor prescribed a gel to reduce inflammation and swelling.`

## Software-team collocations

- `pair programming`, `code review sessions`, `an outdated tech stack`.
- `adopt a four-day work week`, `set up a mentoring scheme`, `new hires`.
- `push back on a proposal`: возразить или сопротивляться предложению.
