**Do your homework:** Be well-prepared; research or practice before doing something.  
*Before the interview, she really did her homework and learned all about the company*  
**Green light:** Official permission to start something.  
*The boss gave us the green light to start the new project*  
**Hit the ground running**: To start a new job or task quickly and with energy, without needing much training or preparation.  
*She really hit the ground running in her new role—by the end of the first week, she had already led two meetings and solved a key issue.*  
**At your fingertips:** Easily available or accessible.  
*With smartphones, so much information is at your fingertips these days.*  
**The personal touch:** A customized or human approach that makes something feel special.  
*The hotel added the personal touch by leaving handwritten notes in the rooms*  
**To come up**: To arise or occur, especially unexpectedly.  
*A problem or opportunity that appears during work or a project.*  
*A last-minute issue came up during the client meeting, so we had to delay the presentation.*  
**Go with the flow:** Adapt easily; not resist change or challenges.  
*When traveling, it’s best to go with the flow if your plans change unexpectedly.*  
**Get carried away:** Become overly enthusiastic or excited, often losing control.  
*They got carried away during the debate and started shouting.*  
**Up your sleeve:** To have a secret plan or idea that you’re saving for later.  
*Don’t worry, he has a few tricks up his sleeve for the presentation*  
**Mind goes blank:** Forget everything suddenly, especially under pressure.  
*During the exam, my mind went blank, and I forgot everything I studied.*  
**Be ahead of the game:** To be more prepared or advanced than others.  
*By learning AI tools early, she’s ahead of the game in her career.*  
**To move forward**: To make progress or continue with the next step in a process.  
*Now that we’ve received management approval, we can move forward with the product launch.*  
**To pay off**: To result in success or a positive outcome after effort or investment.  
*All those late nights working on the proposal really paid off when we won the contract.*

 He finished by telling his manager that all we need is ​the **green light** from you to get started. Despite being so prepared, he had to remind himself to **go with the flow** and to stay calm. In the past, he often got **carried** **away** in his excitement over his ideas.
