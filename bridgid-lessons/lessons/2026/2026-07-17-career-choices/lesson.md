**Career Choices**    
**Idioms** 

**going through the motions:** doing something without energy, interest or enthusiasm, just because you have to  
*By Friday afternoon I'm just going through the motions.*  
**have a calling:** feel strongly that a particular job or type of work is what you're meant to do  
*She always felt teaching was her calling.*  
**fall into a career (by chance):** end up in a job/career without planning it, often by accident or circumstance  
*I never planned to work in finance, I just fell into it.*  
**a big fish in a small pond:** an important or influential person, but only within a small organisation or group  
*He's a big fish in a small pond at that startup.*  
**swim with the sharks:** operate in a highly competitive, sometimes ruthless environment  
*If you want to work in corporate law, you have to be ready to swim with the sharks*.  
**take a leap of faith:** make a risky decision without being sure of the outcome  
*Quitting my job to freelance was a real leap of faith.*  
**pay off:** result in success or benefit, especially after taking a risk or working hard  
*The leap of faith paid off, she loves her new career.*  
**a jack of all trades (master of none):** someone who can do many different things reasonably well but isn't a specialist in any one  
*He's a jack of all trades, he can code, design, and manage projects.*

**My Career \- Idioms**   
**sharks faith calling fish jack motions**

1. Have you ever felt like you were just **going through the motions** at work?  
2. Would you say you **have a calling**, or did you **fall into** your career by chance?  
3. Is it better to be a **big fish in a small pond**, or would you rather **swim with the sharks?**  
4. Have you ever taken a career **leap of faith**? Did it **pay off**?  
5. Do you think it's better to be a **jack of all trades** or to master one skill?
