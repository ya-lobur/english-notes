# Lesson: Geography articles and advanced phrasal verbs

Материалы урока: [Grammar](grammar.md) · [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md) · [Idioms and phrasal verbs](idioms.md)

## Коротко об уроке

- Главные темы: артикли в географических названиях и большой набор advanced phrasal verbs.
- Что уже получается: исходный geography homework помечен `All good`.
- Главный приоритет: учить phrasal verbs вместе с типичным дополнением и ситуацией, а не через русский перевод.
- Авторство пометок: жирным указаны целевые выражения и ответы преподавателя.

## Исправления и уточнения

| Original                                | Better English                   | Тип         | Объяснение                                                                                      |
|-----------------------------------------|----------------------------------|-------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `Is having (a) thick skin...`           | `Is having thick skin...`        | grammar     | В устойчивом значении чаще zero article: `have thick skin`; также возможно `have a thick skin`. |
| `is worth doing`                        | без изменений                    | grammar     | После `worth` употребляется `-ing`, не infinitive.                                              |
| `Stand up for:.`                        | `stand up for`                   | typo        | Двоеточие и точка вместе — опечатка в заметках.                                                 |
| `whittle down the tasks`                | `whittle down the list of tasks` | naturalness | Обычно уменьшают список/число вариантов, а не сами задачи.                                      |
| `the mayor and her community mull over` | `...mulled over`                 | grammar     | Рассказ ведётся в прошлом, поэтому здесь естественнее Past Simple.                              |

## Как это слышит носитель

- `A household name` — широко известный бренд/человек, а не буквально имя семьи.
- `Bear with me` — вежливая просьба о терпении; `put up with me` звучало бы гораздо негативнее.
- `Suss out` разговорнее, чем `determine` или `identify`.

3. Выбери выражение для тщательного обдумывания предложения о работе.
4. Исправь: `This option is worth to consider.`
5. Закончи: `Our company stands ...`

## Production practice

1. Опиши изменение, которое ты хотел бы `bring about`, и препятствия, с которыми можно `come up against`.
2. Расскажи о сложном решении, детали которого пришлось `hammer out` и затем `flesh out`.
3. Составь небольшой рассказ о путешествии, правильно употребив шесть географических названий.

**Advanced Phrasal Verbs**

**Bear with**: To be patient with someone or tolerate a difficult situation.  
*Please bear with me while I try to find the information you need.*

**Bring about**: To cause or make something happen. *Her leadership brought about significant changes in the
organisation.*

**Come up against:** to face a difficulty, problem, or opposition. *We came up against several unexpected challenges
while launching the new product.*

**Flesh out**: To add more details or information to something to make it more complete or comprehensive. *The author
spent months fleshing out the characters in the novel.*

**Hammer out**: To negotiate or work out the details of an agreement or solution through discussion and compromise.
*They had to hammer out a contract that satisfied both parties.*

**Looking back on:** to think about or reflect on something from the past. *Looking back on my childhood, I realise how
lucky I was to have such supportive parents.*

**Mull over**: To carefully consider or think about something for an extended period. *I need some time to mull over the
job offer before making a decision*

**Opt out**: To choose not to participate in something or to withdraw from a situation or commitment. *He decided to opt
out of the project due to time constraints.*

**Pass on:** to give or share information with someone else. *I’ll pass on your message to the manager as soon as she’s
free.*

**Phase out**: To gradually reduce or eliminate something over time. *The company plans to phase out the old software
and replace it with a new system.*

**Stand for:** to support, represent, or embody particular principles, beliefs, or values. *Our organisation stands for
equality, honesty, and respect in the workplace.*

**Stand up for:**. to defend or support someone or something, especially in the face of criticism or opposition. *She
always stands up for her friends when people talk behind their backs.*

**Suss out**: To figure out or understand something through investigation or careful observation. *She managed to suss
out the real reason behind his sudden resignation.*

**Whittle down**: To gradually reduce or decrease something by removing unnecessary parts or elements. *They managed to
whittle down the budget without compromising quality*.
