## Коротко об уроке

Как описывать освоение навыка, нехватку опыта и самостоятельное преодоление трудностей.

Жирное выделение рассматривается как акцент внимания, а не доказательство ошибки или авторства ответа. Авторство отдельных реплик не установлено. Разбор и новые иллюстрации подготовлены Codex.

## Исходные заметки — без изменений

**15 September 2026**  
**SINK OR SWIM**

**be out of your depth:** to be in a situation that's too difficult for you; you lack the knowledge, skill or experience needed  
**live up to (someone's) expectations:** to be as good as people hoped or expected you to be  
**a steep learning curve:** a situation where you have to learn a lot of new things quickly, and it feels hard at first  
**get the hang of (something):** to gradually learn how to do something until it starts to feel easy  
**get by:** to manage to survive or cope with the money, time or ability you have, even if it's limited  
**non-stop:** continuously, without any breaks; extremely busy   
figure out: to understand or solve something by thinking it through  
**sort out:** to deal with a problem successfully, or to organise something messy or confused  
**work out:** to find the solution through thought or calculation; also, if a situation works out,it ends well  
**sink or swim:** a situation where you must succeed through your own effort, with no help or middle ground; you either manage or you fail completely  
**rise to the challenge:** to perform well when faced with a difficult situation, often better than expected
