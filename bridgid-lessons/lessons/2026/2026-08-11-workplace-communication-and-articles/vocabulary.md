# Vocabulary and collocations

[← Lesson overview](lesson.md)

## Swimming

- `freestyle / front crawl`, `backstroke`, `breaststroke`, `butterfly` — swimming strokes.
- `flippers`, `a kickboard`, `swimming goggles` — equipment.
- `do/swim lengths`, `dive into the pool`, `breathing technique` — natural collocations.
- Example: `I swam twenty lengths using freestyle and breaststroke.`

## Communication skills

- `active listening`, `clarity`, `confidence`, `empathy`, `brevity`, `negotiating`.
- `non-verbal communication`: gestures, facial expression, posture and other signals without words.
- `retention`: сохранение сотрудников в компании; `employee retention`.
- `move within/across the company`: перейти внутри компании или между функциями/подразделениями.

## `listen to` vs `hear`

- `listen to` — намеренно направлять внимание на звук.
- `hear` — воспринимать звук, часто без намерения.
- `I listened to her explanation, but I couldn’t hear every word.`
